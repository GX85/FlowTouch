# FlowTouch / Spatial Glass

## Style Prompt
Use the user's three references: the precise white exoskeleton hand and curved forearm, a physical cubic room in perspective, and translucent layered Apple-style controls. Quiet pale mineral surfaces, realistic contact shadows, smoked glass, very restrained warm orange signals. Leave large areas of empty space. Keep the robotic hand's construction intact. No human skin, invented skeleton, neon sci-fi HUD, gradient lettering or decorative emoji.

## Colors
- Canvas / mist: #eef0ed
- Ink / joints: #232823
- Mineral gray: #89948b
- Action / amber: #ba5639
- Glass / frost: #ffffff88

## Typography
Inter Tight (existing local font), system UI fallback. Large compact headlines, readable regular-weight body text. No forced wide uppercase paragraphs.

## Motion
Room camera eases toward the pointer. Fog drifts slowly. Rain is opt-in and silent. Controls have 160–240 ms mechanical snaps; page entrances ease over 600 ms. Respect reduced motion. Pause offscreen rendering. Cap device pixel ratio at 1.5 and use one canvas.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not turn 126 command patterns into a claim of 126 distinct hand poses.
- Do not simulate recognition or display invented success metrics.
- Do not advertise a bitmap as a rigged 3D model.
- Do not start audio, camera, rain or cloud analysis automatically.
- Do not bundle a website as the downloadable product; Studio opens its own workspace.
